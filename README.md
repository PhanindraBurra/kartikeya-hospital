### 🚀 Key Features
- **Cinematic Introductory Section:** Welcomes visitors with the hospital building photo, mission, and live badges.
- **Interactive Smart Chatbox ("HealthBot"):** Responds to questions in Telugu and English, with direct WhatsApp escalation.
- **Appointment Booking System:** Generates digital token IDs and allows one-click dispatch to the hospital's official WhatsApp (`+91 9391131013`).
- **Instagram Showcase Hub:** Follow section with reels, health tips, and direct social links (`@kartikeyahospital`).
- **High-Resolution Poster & Brochure Lightbox:** View and zoom original Telugu posters.
- **Bilingual Switcher:** Toggle easily between English and Telugu.
- **Mobile Responsive & Accessible:** Optimized for smartphones, tablets, and desktops.
